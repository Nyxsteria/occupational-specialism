Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how this website uses cookies and similar technologies to improve user experience, remember user preferences, and support core website functionality.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit, such as preferences, session information, and settings.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies are required for the website to function correctly. These cookies cannot be disabled as they are necessary for security, authentication, and session management.

Preference Cookies

Preference cookies remember choices you make, such as theme settings, accessibility options, and other customisations, to provide a more personalised experience.

Session Cookies

Session cookies maintain information while you navigate between pages and are automatically removed when your browsing session ends.

How We Use Cookies

  • To maintain secure user sessions.
  • To remember login preferences.
  • To improve website performance.
  • To enhance user experience.
  • To store consent preferences.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through browser settings. You can choose to block, delete, or receive notifications before cookies are stored. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.
